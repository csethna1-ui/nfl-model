## V2 autonomous research — final verdict (2026-09-29): V2_UNPROVEN

The full V2 ladder ran end-to-end under the frozen protocol. V2-A (component),
V2-B (+matchup interactions), V2-C (+QB/player), V2-D (+possession/
explosiveness/pressure) all froze α=100.0 and all are **significantly worse
than V1** on 2021–2022 REG (543 games): MAEs 10.555 / 10.573 / 10.395 / 10.673
vs V1 10.059, paired bootstrap p = 0.0042 / 0.0018 / 0.0236 / 0.0006, all in
V1's favor. G1/G2 failed on all four. Error correlations with V1 (0.946–0.959)
confirm the V2s were genuinely different models, not V1 twins — just worse.

No candidate passed the gates, so V2-E was not built, the 2023–2025 vault
stays pristine (never touched), and no V2_FINAL_* files were produced.
V1 remains production, unmodified. Full report: v2_research/V2_RESEARCH_REPORT.md.
